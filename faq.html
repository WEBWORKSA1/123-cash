FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is 123.Cash free to use?

Yes — every calculator, guide and the Cash Plan are free.

Are you a lender or bank?

No. We're an independent publisher. We never make credit decisions or hold funds.

Does getting a Cash Plan affect my credit score?

No. We don't run credit checks. A lender you choose to apply with later may.

Do the calculators store my data?

No. They run entirely in your browser.

How do contests work?

Free, skill-judged monthly contests. See official rules.

Where do donations go?

Operations, marketing, hiring talent and prizes — see the breakdown on the Support page.

Can I advertise or partner?

Yes — see Advertise and Partners.

Is this site related to other "123 Cash" businesses?

No. See our Trademark & Copyright notice.
