About

Money made as simple as 1-2-3

Most money advice is either too complicated or quietly selling something. 123.Cash exists to fix that: pick a goal, run the numbers, take one clear next step.

Our principles

  • Numbers first. Every guide links to a calculator so you can check the math yourself.
  • Plain English. If a 14-year-old can't follow it, we rewrite it.
  • Transparent money. We earn from ads, sponsors, partner referrals and reader support — and we always label it.
  • No predatory products. We don't promote products we wouldn't recommend to family.

Editorial standards

Guides are researched from primary sources (regulators, central banks, official statistics), reviewed for accuracy and updated when rules change. Found an error? Tell us — corrections are published promptly.

How we make money

Display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense), sponsorships, affiliate and lead-referral partnerships, and voluntary reader support. Compensation may influence where offers appear, but never our calculators' math or our editorial opinions.
