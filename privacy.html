Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Summary

  • Calculators run in your browser; we don't receive what you type into them.
  • We collect information you choose to submit in forms (e.g. name, email, goal).
  • With your consent, we use cookies for analytics and advertising (Google AdSense / Analytics).
  • We never sell your personal information. We share lead details only with partners relevant to your stated goal, and only when you consent.

Information we collect

You provide: form submissions (Cash Plan, contact, contest, donation pledge, job application, newsletter). Automatically: standard technical data (IP address, browser, pages visited) via hosting logs and, with consent, analytics and advertising cookies.

How we use it

To respond to requests, deliver your Cash Plan, run contests, process pledges, evaluate applications, send newsletters you asked for, improve the site, prevent fraud and comply with law.

Service providers

Hosting (GitHub Pages), form delivery (FormSubmit), analytics (Google Analytics), advertising (Google AdSense), video (YouTube, privacy-enhanced mode) and payment processors you choose (e.g. Stripe, PayPal). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Google advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Québec Law 25, India DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, withdraw consent, or opt out of sale/sharing. Submit requests through our contact form.

Retention & security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 16, and contests are 18+.

Changes

We'll post updates here with a new "last updated" date.
