25 Side Hustles Ranked by Real Hourly Pay

By 123.Cash Editorial · Educational content, not financial advice

The best side hustle is the one you'll actually start. We scored each idea on startup cost, time to first dollar, skills needed and scalability. Pay ranges are typical, not guaranteed.

Skill-based (highest hourly pay)

  1. Freelance writing/editing — $20–$75/hr. Build 3 samples, pitch 10 clients weekly.
  2. Web design & development — $30–$100/hr.
  3. Bookkeeping — $25–$60/hr; recurring monthly clients.
  4. Online tutoring — $20–$60/hr.
  5. Graphic design — $25–$75/hr.
  6. Video editing — $25–$60/hr; creators are always hiring.
  7. AI automation services — set up chatbots, workflows and content pipelines for small businesses.
  8. Translation — $0.08–$0.20 per word.

Fast-start (cash in days)

  1. Food & grocery delivery
  2. Rideshare driving
  3. Pet sitting & dog walking
  4. House cleaning
  5. Moving help & odd jobs
  6. Reselling thrift & marketplace finds
  7. Event staffing
  8. User testing websites & apps

Asset-building (slower, scales)

  1. YouTube channel
  2. Niche content websites
  3. Digital templates & printables
  4. Online courses
  5. Newsletter with sponsorships
  6. Print-on-demand
  7. Stock photography & video
  8. Domain investing
  9. Affiliate marketing

How to choose in 3 steps

1. Need cash this month? Pick a fast-start hustle. 2. Have a marketable skill? Freelance it. 3. Playing the long game? Build an asset. Then run your numbers in the side-hustle income calculator — remember to set aside money for taxes.
